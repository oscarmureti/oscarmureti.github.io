I bought "Remove ads" and the ads came back

Open Settings → Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple or Google account, so this also brings them back on a new device. If it still does not work, email the receipt and it will be sorted out.

I lost my progress

Progress is stored only on your device — there is no account and nothing on a server. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes it permanently and it cannot be recovered. Cloud saves are being considered for a future update.

My streak reset even though I played

A streak counts calendar days in your device's own time zone. Travelling across time zones, or changing the device clock, can end a run. A streak freeze from the daily gift bridges exactly one missed day.

A word was rejected that I am sure is real

Wordroot accepts the ENABLE lexicon, the standard open word list used by word games. It is broad but not infinite. Send the word and it will be looked at.

Is this Solitaire deal actually winnable?

Yes. Every shipped deal was proven beatable by a solver before release. If you are stuck, the hint button runs that same solver on your current board and tells you a move that still leads to a win.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. Twelve languages are available, and the default follows your device.

How do I stop personalised ads?

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland: Settings → Privacy options. Elsewhere, use your device controls — iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or Android Settings → Google → Ads.
