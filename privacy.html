Privacy Policy

Applies to Wordroot, Solitaire Assured and Sudoku Mentor.

HomeSupport

Last updated: 10 September 2026

The short version

These games have no accounts, no sign-in and no server of our own. We do not know who you are. Your progress, settings and purchases are stored only on your device.

The one exception is advertising: the games show ads supplied by Google AdMob, and Google receives some information in order to serve them.

What stays on your device

All of it, and none of it reaches us:

  • Puzzle progress, levels completed and daily-streak history
  • Coins, hints, reveal tokens and other in-game items
  • Your language, theme and difficulty settings
  • Whether you have purchased the ad-free upgrade

Uninstalling the app deletes this. We hold no copy, so we cannot restore it.

What Google AdMob collects

We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob is operated by Google and may collect:

  • A device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS, and only on iOS if you allow tracking when asked)
  • Device information such as model, operating-system version and language
  • Coarse location derived from your IP address
  • Interactions with ads, such as views and taps

We never see this data ourselves, and we cannot connect it to a person. It is governed by Google's own policies: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Your choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we show a consent form before any personalised ad is requested. Choose non-personalised ads and Google will not use the advertising identifier to profile you. You can revisit the choice any time from Settings → Privacy options.
  • On iOS we ask separately, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, before the IDFA can be used. Decline and it is not used. Change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Buying the ad-free upgrade stops all banner, interstitial and app-open ads. Rewarded videos remain available only if you choose to watch one.

Purchases

The ad-free upgrade is processed by Apple or Google. We never receive or store your payment details. All we record on your device is whether the purchase was made.

Children

These games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. They are not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

Data we do not collect

To be explicit: no names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, files or health data. There is no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service and no social login.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
